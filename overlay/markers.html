Calibration markers
print, cut out, tape to the cloth
Back

Four markers, one for each corner. They let the camera calibrate itself to about a millimetre — better than anyone can manage by clicking — and if you leave them taped down, a camera that gets knocked can notice and fix itself.

  1. Print this page at 100% scale. Do not let the printer "fit to page": the markers do not have to be an exact size, but they do have to stay square.
  2. Cut out each marker leaving the white border on. The white is not decoration — the detector needs it to find the black edge.
  3. Tape each one flat on the cloth just inside the corner it names, with its outer corner on the cushion corner.
  4. Then Setup → Camera → Calibrate from markers.

Leave them there. They are out of the way at the very corners, and they are what lets the table recover from being nudged.

Nothing below this line prints except the four markers.
